## Definition
The recurring public and parliamentary debate over whether National Service allowances constitute fair compensation, versus [[mindef|MINDEF]]'s position that NS is a civic duty rather than a compensable transaction. Surfaced in the 27 Feb 2026 Committee of Supply debate (WP MP [[kenneth-tiong|Kenneth Tiong]]'s proposal to peg second-year NSF allowances to the Local Qualifying Salary, rejected by [[minister-for-defence|Defence Minister]] [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]]) and amplified by former [[rsaf|RSAF]] pilot [[jonathan-tee|Jonathan Tee]]'s public criticism.
