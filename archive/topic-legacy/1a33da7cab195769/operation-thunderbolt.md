## Definition
The [[saf|SAF]] Special Operations Force's 30-second storming of hijacked [[singapore-airlines|Singapore Airlines]] Flight SQ117 on 27 Mar 1991 at [[changi-airport|Changi Airport]], killing all four hijackers and rescuing 123 hostages with no fatalities — Singapore's only Singapore Airlines hijacking and one of its most notable counter-terrorism successes.
