## Definition
Annual bilateral live-firing armour exercise between the [[singapore-army|Singapore Army]] (48th Battalion, Singapore Armoured Regiment) and the [[bundeswehr|German Army]] at the [[oberlausitz|Oberlausitz Military Training Area]], Germany. The 2026 edition marked its 10th anniversary.
