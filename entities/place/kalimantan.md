## Summary
Region of Indonesian Borneo where Indonesia is preparing a joint military training facility specifically for Singapore, announced Mar 2026 by Indonesian Defence Minister [[sjafrie-sjamsoeddin|Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin]] — an alternative to Morotai, which Singapore had earlier rejected as too distant. Discussions trace to an Aug 2025 Jakarta meeting between Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] and GEN Sjafrie.
