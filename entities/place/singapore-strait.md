## Summary
International shipping lane between Singapore and Indonesia's Riau Islands, including the 2.8km-wide Philips Channel, protected under UNCLOS as a Strait Used for International Navigation with guaranteed transit passage rights — a route for both allied naval exercises and sanctioned "dark fleet" tankers.
