## Summary
[[rsaf|RSAF]] air base, distinct from the adjacent civilian [[changi-airport|Changi Airport]]. RSAF A330 MRTT repatriation flights to Riyadh departed from and returned to Changi Air Base during the Mar 2026 Middle East evacuation operation.
