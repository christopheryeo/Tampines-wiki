## Summary
One of the SAF's live-firing and demolition islands (with Pulau Senang and Pulau Pawai) in Singapore's southern waters. SAF conducted live-firing drills there from 30 Mar to 6 Apr 2026, alongside exercises at the Pasir Laba (SAFTI) Live-Firing Area and across several mainland training areas.
