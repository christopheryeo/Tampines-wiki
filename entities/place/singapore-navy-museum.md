## Summary
Museum within [[changi-naval-base|Changi Naval Base]] chronicling the [[rsn|RSN]]'s evolution from early wooden vessels to a modern maritime force. Originated as a naval archives facility (1987), renamed Navy Museum (1989), originally sited at Sembawang Camp before relocating to Changi Naval Base (reopened 2012, renovated 2022).
