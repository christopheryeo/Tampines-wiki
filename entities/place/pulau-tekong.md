## Summary
Island where three BMTC (Basic Military Training Centre) schools are located; NSF recruits training
here return via Pasir Ris MRT on weekends when booked out, via the SAF Ferry Terminal serving the
island. Also one of the outdoor live-firing range sites (alongside Nee Soon and Poyan) that will remain after MMRC 2 opens at [[bedok-camp|Bedok Camp]].
