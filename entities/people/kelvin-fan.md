## Summary
Major-General, Chief of the [[rsaf|Republic of Singapore Air Force]] since 22 Mar 2024, succeeding Kelvin Khong; promoted to major-general on 1 Jul 2024. Previously Deputy Secretary (Policy) at [[mindef|MINDEF]], and has held roles including Commanding Officer of 163 Squadron, Head of Air Plans/Air Operations, Director of the Defence Policy Office, and Commander, Air Defence and Operations Command.
