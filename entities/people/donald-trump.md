## Summary
US President. A week into the March 2026 Iran war, escalated by demanding Iran's "unconditional surrender" and a say in choosing its next leader after Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was killed in the war's opening strikes, rattling global markets. Feuded publicly with UK PM Keir Starmer, rejecting a UK offer of carrier support and citing London's initial refusal to let the US use its bases against Iran. Has a Beijing visit planned for 31 Mar–2 Apr 2026 (later narrowed to Beijing only), the first sitting US president visit to China since 2017, with China's Wang Yi framing Trump-Xi ties as stabilising ahead of it. Received a call from Vladimir Putin proposing a quick diplomatic end to the Iran conflict; the two also discussed a possible Ukraine ceasefire.
