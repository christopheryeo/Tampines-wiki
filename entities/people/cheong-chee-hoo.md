## Summary
Chief Executive Officer of DSO National Laboratories, Singapore's national defence research and development organisation. Joined DSO in 1993; career progression included Deputy Chief R&T Officer at [[mindef|MINDEF]] (2009–2012), Deputy Director at [[dsta|DSTA]] (2008–2009), and Deputy Chief Executive Officer (Systems) at DSO from Oct 2014, before becoming CEO. Background spans radar, satellite, systems design and systems engineering.
