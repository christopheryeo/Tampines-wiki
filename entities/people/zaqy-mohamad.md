## Summary
SMS for Defence (also holds Sustainability and Environment). Spoke at the opening ceremony of the inaugural Defence Collective Festival (Nov 2025, marking 60 years of the SAF) at the Singapore Discovery Centre, on nurturing future generations to value what NSmen, NSFs, and their families have built. In his 27 Feb 2026 COS speech to Parliament, raised MINDEF's Work-While-Learning Scheme (~850 NSFs across 10 schemes), announced the [[sg-defence-volunteer-network|SG Defence Volunteer Network]], and stressed Singapore would continue strengthening its cyber defence capabilities. Attended German Defence Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]]'s Istana call on PM Lawrence Wong (Mar 2026).
