## Summary
Singapore's Defence Minister (also holds Coordinating Minister for Public Services — a pairing spanning hard security and social policy). On 31 Oct 2025, called for deeper regional defence cooperation at a dinner with China's Minister of Defence on the sidelines of the 19th [[admm|ADMM]] in [[kuala-lumpur|Kuala Lumpur]]; also met India's Minister of Defence and was scheduled to attend ADMM-Plus on 1 Nov. On 23 Nov, visited Navy@Vivo 2025 at [[vivocity|VivoCity]], calling defence "a whole-of-nation effort". On 26 Nov, gave the opening speech at the 26th [[acamm|ACAMM]] in Singapore. On 17 Mar 2026, in his Public Services capacity, spoke at Singapore World Water Day on strengthening water resilience via the Singapore Water Saver Programme. In Mar 2026, announced G550 procurement and MMRC 2, fielded multiple parliamentary written replies, rejected [[kenneth-tiong|Kenneth Tiong]]'s NSF-allowance proposal (drawing public criticism from [[jonathan-tee|Jonathan Tee]] and [[terry-xu|Terry Xu]]), warned on Strait of Hormuz risk, commissioned 253 new officers, held calls/meetings with [[qatar|Qatar]]'s DPM, German Defence Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]], and US CNO [[daryl-caudle|Daryl Caudle]].

Personally favours direct engagement over formal channels — phoned Qatar's defence minister himself over the Strait of Hormuz blockade risk (13 Mar), warning it would disrupt supply chains for critical materials like helium and urea. Has a decades-long personal friendship with Indonesia's defence minister [[sjafrie-sjamsoeddin|Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin]], dating to when both were young officers ([[singapore-army|Singapore Army]] and Kopassus respectively) — now shaping a new joint Singapore training facility in [[kalimantan|Kalimantan]]. His public rhetoric leans values-first and long-horizon rather than technocratic: framed Singapore's falling birth rate as a values question rather than a cost-benefit one (13 Mar charity dinner), called for supporting women balancing career and family as men take on more at home, and invoked the Global Financial Crisis and COVID-19 as touchstones for national unity amid AI-driven volatility and Middle East-driven supply shocks (14 Mar, [[singapore-kwangtung-hui-kuan|Singapore Kwangtung Hui Kuan]] speech) — summed up in his own words: "one minute on stage requires ten years of effort behind the scenes."
