## Summary
One of few Singaporeans to complete US Navy SEAL training; as an [[saf|SAF]] Special Operations Force commando, took part in the 30-second assault that ended the 1991 hijacking of Singapore Airlines Flight SQ117 at [[changi-airport|Changi Airport]]. Retired from the SAF in 2013 after 32 years of service, later ordained a Buddhist monk by the Dalai Lama in Dharamsala, India (2017), taking the name Venerable Tenzin Drachom.
