## Summary
Brigadier-General, an air force officer who assumed the Chief of Staff (Joint Staff) appointment in Apr 2025, succeeding BG Goh Pei Ming. Concurrently holds the SAF Inspector-General portfolio (compliance, training standards, operational readiness audits) and Chief Sustainability Officer portfolio (integrating environmental resilience into defence practices).
