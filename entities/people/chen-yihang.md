## Summary
15-year-old who became Singapore's first professional Weiqi (Go) player on 31 Mar 2026, inducted at the Nihon Ki-in (Japan Go Association) in Tokyo after winning the Winter Professional Qualification League in Nov 2025. Will step away from the Japanese professional circuit for at least four years, returning to Singapore to complete his International Baccalaureate at Anglo-Chinese School (Independent) and fulfil his NS obligations before reapplying in 2030.
