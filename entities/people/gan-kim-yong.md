## Summary
Singapore's Deputy Prime Minister. During the 26 Feb 2026 Committee of Supply debate, said the resident total fertility rate fell to a record-low 0.87 in 2025 (down from 0.97 in 2024), warned the citizen population could start shrinking by the early 2040s, and said fewer citizens would make it increasingly difficult to meet national security and defence manpower needs. Also announced Singapore would grant 25,000-30,000 citizenships annually over the next five years via "carefully managed immigration flow".
