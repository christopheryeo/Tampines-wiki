## Summary
Singaporean blogger who travelled to the US in 2016 and was granted asylum in 2017; later imprisoned there for sex offences (sentenced Dec 2021), re-arrested for parole violation in 2023, and deported back to Singapore on 20 Mar 2026. Arrested by [[cmpb|CMPB]] at [[changi-airport|Changi Airport]] and charged for failing to report for pre-enlistment medical examinations and remaining overseas without a valid exit permit. Granted S$10,000 bail on 26 Mar 2026 (barred from public comment on his case) and released from Changi Prison the same day; allegedly emailed plans for a Gandhi-inspired "nonviolent movement."
