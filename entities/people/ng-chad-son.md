## Summary
Chief Executive of [[dsta|DSTA]] since 15 May 2024, succeeding Mervyn Tan Wei Ming. Previously Deputy Secretary (Technology)/Future Systems and Technology Architect at [[mindef|MINDEF]]; earlier career appointments include Director of Military Intelligence and Chief of Command, Control, Communications, Computer and Intelligence (C4I) in the SAF, and Director of the Research and Enterprise Division at the Ministry of Trade and Industry.
