## Summary
69-year-old general who commanded Myanmar's armed forces since 2011 and led the 2021 coup. Stepped down on 30 Mar 2026 to stand as a vice-presidential candidate in the first parliamentary polls since the coup, handing the commander-in-chief position to [[ye-win-oo|Ye Win Oo]] at a ceremony in [[naypyitaw|Naypyitaw]].
