## Summary
US Chief of Naval Operations (CNO). Made his first visit to Singapore since taking the post in 2025, a three-day trip from 25 Mar 2026; met Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]], inspected a Guard of Honour at [[mindef|MINDEF]], called on CDF [[aaron-beng|VADM Aaron Beng]] and Chief of Navy [[sean-wat|RADM Sean Wat]], and visited Changi Naval Base to review [[rsn|RSN]] maritime security developments including unmanned capabilities. At Naval Support Activity Singapore (25 Mar), said the Military Sealift Command's staffing and fleet-age issues were his greatest worry, and outlined US Navy plans for dedicated unmanned-systems task forces.
