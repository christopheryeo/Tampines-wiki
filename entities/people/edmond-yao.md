## Summary
Singapore-born man granted Indonesian citizenship as a child, convicted on 23 Mar 2026 of failing to report for NS enlistment in Jan 1997. Argued Indonesian law barred him from foreign military service; the court rejected this, ruling he could not "cherry-pick" which citizenship's obligations to follow given he remained a Singapore citizen by birth. Sentencing adjourned to Apr 2026; faces up to 3 years' jail, a S$5,000 fine, or both.
