Princess Royal of the UK. Visited Singapore for two days from 12 Nov 2025 to mark the 60th anniversary of Singapore–UK diplomatic relations; met PM Lawrence Wong at the Istana, and visited the Airbus Asia Training Centre and Rolls-Royce's Singapore facilities.
