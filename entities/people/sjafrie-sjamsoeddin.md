## Summary
Indonesia's Defence Minister. Announced 12 Mar 2026 that Indonesia is preparing a joint military training facility in North [[kalimantan|Kalimantan]] for Singapore, after Singapore rejected an earlier offer of Morotai (deemed too far). Met Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] in Jakarta in Aug 2025 to discuss joint training facilities in Kalimantan, Baturaja, and the Siabu Air Weapons Range; the two have known each other since both were young officers.
