## Summary
[[workers-party|Workers' Party]] MP. During the 27 Feb 2026 Committee of Supply debate, proposed aligning second-year NSF allowances with the Local Qualifying Salary (S$1,800/month), arguing duty and compensation were not incompatible since SAF regulars serve the same mission on market-rate pay; Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] rejected the proposal on principle. Also asked Mr Chan in a written parliamentary question about defence contracts awarded via closed vs open tender.
