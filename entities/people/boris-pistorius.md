## Summary
Germany's Defence Minister. Made an official visit to Singapore 23-25 Mar 2026, meeting PM [[lawrence-wong|Lawrence Wong]] at the Istana and Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] at The Fullerton Hotel; witnessed the signing of a Supply Chain Resiliency Arrangement and a [[tkms|TKMS]]/[[st-engineering|ST Engineering]] submarine-maintenance MoU. Presented Germany's Order of Merit to former Defence Minister [[ng-eng-hen|Ng Eng Hen]], and visited [[dsta|DSTA]]'s Quantum Sensing Center and Changi Naval Base.
