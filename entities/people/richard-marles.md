## Summary
Australia's Defence Minister. In Feb 2026, said the aim of selling up to 67 surplus Australian Defence Force sites (an audit valued them at ~A$3 billion) was to consolidate holdings and fund housing, given some sites' high maintenance costs — a plan defence experts warned could hurt training capacity and morale.
