## Definition
Singapore's national movement (since 2016) to build community vigilance against terrorism, sensitising, training and mobilising Singaporeans to prevent, detect and respond to a terror attack. The "Play Your Role" campaign, launched 5 Mar 2026 at Bishan Junction 8, is the latest roadshow under this movement.
