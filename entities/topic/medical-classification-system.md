## Definition
MINDEF/SAF's framework for assessing personnel medical fitness for national service roles, revised in 2026 to move beyond a binary combat-worthy/non-combat-worthy split so personnel with health limitations but relevant technical skills (e.g. cyber) can still be classified as deployable.
