## Definition
Umbrella topic for coverage of national defence as a whole — military capability, defence policy and strategy, armed forces organisation, and the protection of national sovereignty. Use only when an article addresses defence broadly; prefer a narrower topic (e.g. procurement, exercises, NS policy) when one applies.

## Notes
Broad parent theme for the wiki's defence-related vocabulary. Narrower existing topics and categories that roll up conceptually under it include Defence Policy (e.g. [[medical-classification-system|Medical Classification System (MCS)]], [[ns-bullying-harassment|NS Bullying/Harassment Incidents]]), Defence Procurement (e.g. [[sa26|SA26 (Singapore Airshow 2026)]]), defence diplomacy agreements (e.g. [[edca|Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement (EDCA)]]), and Singapore's Total Defence framework ([[total-defence-day|Total Defence Day]]).

In the Singapore monitoring context, defence spans the SAF's three services and the Digital and Intelligence Service, MINDEF policy, National Service, defence technology and procurement (DSTA, DSO), defence diplomacy, and Total Defence's six pillars (military, civil, economic, social, psychological, digital).
