#### Lessons learned

- **The provider's date field is UTC; the plan's is local.** A same-day Singapore crawl launched in
  the early SGT hours submits a UTC day that has not started, so every topic returns a false zero.
  This is a silent failure: the provider answers `200` with `totalResults: 0`, which is
  indistinguishable from genuine no-coverage. A control keyword (`Trump`) returning zero for the
  same window is a cheap, decisive check and should be standard.
- **The offset costs recall on every run, not just early ones.** Even a mid-day crawl submitting
  local dates verbatim silently drops the 00:00–08:00 SGT slice of the day.
- **Date-sorted paging makes a narrow window cheap.** Because `articlesSortBy: "date"` returns newest
  first, every one of the 92 groups covered the whole window within a single page — 92 provider
  calls for 96 topics.
- **Judging topic fit and the vault bar in one question is too permissive.** The combined gate passed
  121 articles, including routine Ukraine strike reports and AP "what to know" explainers that the
  standing bar excludes. Re-asking the bar as its own question, independent of topic, dropped 90 of
  those 121. Topic fit and mission fit are separate judgements and score better asked separately.
- **Grouping identical keyword arrays is free recall.** Five Singapore-specific topics resolved to
  the same `Singapore` pool; collapsing them saved four provider calls with no loss.

#### Recommendations to improve the topic crawl plan

- **Evidence:** submitting `dateStart = dateEnd = 2026-09-18` (local) at 03:00 SGT returned
  `totalResults: 0` for every tested keyword, including a `Trump` control, because the UTC day
  2026-09-18 had not begun; the provider's newest indexed article was 2026-09-17T19:00:00Z.
  Converting to the overlapping UTC window and applying the Step 7 gate returned 6,183 in-window
  candidates and ultimately 31 cascaded articles.
- **Recommended change:** state in Step 3 that NewsAPI.ai `dateStart`/`dateEnd` select UTC days,
  require conversion of the local window to the overlapping UTC days before the call, and keep the
  Step 7 timezone date gate as the authority over the widened result set.
- **Implementation:** applied to `scripts/topic_crawl_plan.md` on branch
  `maintenance/provider-utc-date-window` — a new "Provider date window (required)" rule in Step 3
  with this run's evidence, a clarified `dateStart`/`dateEnd` invocation input, and a matching End
  Conditions checkbox. Opened as a PR; not merged by this run.
- **Expected accuracy benefit:** removes a silent whole-run false zero for same-day Singapore crawls
  and recovers the 00:00–08:00 SGT slice that every day-bounded crawl currently drops.
- **Expected OpenAI API-call reduction:** none directly — the change is a provider-query correction.
  It is strongly call-*positive* in the sense that it prevents a wasted run; the separate headline
  triage pre-gate in this run cut the source-body gate from a would-be 3,448-article workload to
  321, about a 91% reduction in relevance-model input volume.
