#### Lessons learned

- **An intelligence chief's on-record parliamentary testimony clears the top-tier-regional bar even
  without a kinetic event.** Today's second accepted item was a formal warning, not an attack or
  agreement — but a named national spy chief testifying to a parliamentary oversight committee that
  his country risks being drawn into war is a concrete, attributable state-level assessment, not
  punditry. The same keyword-assisted shortlist surfaced several superficially similar "warning"
  headlines (Iranian commanders, Finland's president) that were correctly dropped as routine
  commentary on an already-known situation — the distinguishing test was whether the statement itself
  was the day's new development (BND chief, yes — first such parliamentary testimony reported) versus
  restating an established position (Iran's FM repeating "no military solution" for the eighth month
  running).
- **A long-running, already-massively-covered war needs an explicit "no new development" check, not
  just a volume cap.** `iran-regional-conflict`'s 6,601 pass-2 results correctly triggered the
  high-volume-untriaged scope limit, but three narrow-topic candidates (via `maritime-security`,
  mostly) still surfaced routine Iran-war coverage that had to be individually read and dropped. A
  topic already carrying an established, ongoing, multi-month conflict is structurally guaranteed to
  keep generating incidental hits in unrelated narrow topics' result sets; today's bar correctly
  filtered these at the manual-review step, but a cheap pre-filter (e.g., flagging candidates whose
  body text frames itself as continuing an already-dated conflict, "since late February") could save
  review time on future high-Iran-volume days.
- **A single real event can out-rank its topic's own keyword query.** The Black Sea ship strike was
  discovered via the `russia-ukraine-war` narrow topic's own displayName query, but the Taiwan
  assessment-program story surfaced only via `military-exercises`/`defence-policy-strategy`, and the
  Panama Canal story only via `maritime-security` — none of these came from a topic built specifically
  around them (there is no "Taiwan Strait" or "Singapore-Panama" narrow query that caught them first).
  This reinforces the 2026-10-05 finding that keeping the per-topic query net wide matters more than
  narrowing for precision.
