## Question
What has Chan Chun Sing been doing?

## Answer
Chan Chun Sing is Singapore's Defence Minister (also Coordinating Minister for Public Services). His recent activity on file:

Defence diplomacy — On 31 Oct 2025 he called for deeper regional defence cooperation at a dinner with China's Minister of Defence on the sidelines of the 19th ADMM in Kuala Lumpur, also met India's Minister of Defence, and was scheduled to attend ADMM-Plus on 1 Nov (757551). He held calls/meetings with Qatar's Deputy PM (1013411, 1015296), German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius (1029327), and the US Chief of Naval Operations Daryl Caudle (1032776).

Public engagements & ceremonies — Visited Navy@Vivo 2025 at VivoCity on 23 Nov 2025, calling defence "a whole-of-nation effort" (801234); gave the opening speech at the 26th ACAMM in Singapore on 26 Nov 2025 (810798); spoke at Singapore World Water Day on 17 Mar 2026 in his Public Services capacity on water resilience and the Singapore Water Saver Programme (1018160); and commissioned 253 new SAF officers (1014225).

Procurement & capability — Announced the G550 patrol aircraft procurement and MMRC 2 (990581), a second SAF indoor live-firing range at Bedok (991349), the Poseidon plan deepening US ties (992123), and a possible TKMS/ST Engineering submarine maintenance hub MoU (1029768).

Parliament & National Service debate — Fielded multiple parliamentary written replies in Mar 2026 on NSmen IPPT results, madrasah students' NS participation, defence expenditure principles, bullying in NS, the Enhanced Expertise Deployment Scheme, and defence contracts/procurement (993651, 993653, 993655, 1000279, 1000420, 1000422); rejected Kenneth Tiong's NSF-allowance proposal, drawing criticism from Jonathan Tee and Terry Xu and a challenge from a former RSAF pilot over his NS remarks (992881, 1001825); and warned on Strait of Hormuz / Iran-conflict risk (992273).
