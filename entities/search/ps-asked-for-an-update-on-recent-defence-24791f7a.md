## Question
PS asked for an update on recent defence related issues (interpreted as: current status of the

## Answer
Two items are hot. The NS Enforcement/Enlistment Act cluster is the most severe: the Amos Yee and
Edmond Yao prosecutions have fused into one internationally-watched test of enlistment enforcement,
with MINDEF/MHA on record taking a firm stand, a pre-trial conference set for 23 Apr, and Yao's
sentencing due in April — both are irreversible on-record positions the next hearings will be
measured against. Also hot: the domestic fallout from the Israel/Gaza conflict — a father-son pair's
past volunteering with the Israeli-linked Sar-El corps drew a joint MHA/MINDEF statement, K.
Shanmugam has staked out zero tolerance in Parliament (prosecution, ISA detention, or loss of
citizenship), and police are investigating a POFMA'd Online Citizen article alongside an unanswered
formal query to Israel — several live fuses still open.

Two are warm. Commentary questioning Singapore's neutrality amid the US-China and US-Israel-Iran
tensions is building in foreign outlets (Hong Kong, Malaysia, Taiwan), reviving "equidistance" and
"lie low" framing — low volume so far but a narrative risk that could force MFA/PM-level response if
it doubles. Separately, the German Defence Minister's March visit produced a Supply Chain Resiliency
Arrangement and a TKMS-ST Engineering submarine service centre MOU — currently positive/facilitated
coverage with no critical fault line yet, but breadth and institutional attachment have surged.

One is on watch: US force posture shifts (Okinawa-based Marines, CENTCOM reinforcement transiting
near Singapore) forced MINDEF onto the record once already about facilitating US transits under the
1990 MoU. The spike has since subsided, but it's worth watching for that domestic-migration pattern
to recur.

(A sixth flagged cluster — a March water/climate coverage spike — was reviewed and dismissed: it
traced entirely to PUB's facilitated World Water Day campaign, with no ramification, and isn't
defence-related.)
