## Question
What was said about Seletar Aerospace Park?

## Answer
Not much was directly "said" about Seletar Aerospace Park — it's mentioned as a location, not a
subject of remarks. The one thing on record: Princess Anne visited the Airbus Asia Training Centre at
Seletar Aerospace Park on 13 Nov 2025, as part of her two-day UK state visit to Singapore, "to learn
about Airbus's innovative technologies and the company's growth in the Asia-Pacific region." This came
right after her meeting with PM Lawrence Wong at the Istana, and just before she went on to visit
Rolls-Royce's Singapore facilities.
