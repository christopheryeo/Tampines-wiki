## Answer
It's the NS allowance debate. At the 27 Feb 2026 Committee of Supply debate, Workers' Party MP
Kenneth Tiong proposed pegging second-year NSF allowances to the Local Qualifying Salary; Defence
Minister Chan Chun Sing rejected it, framing NS as a "sacred duty" that shouldn't be reduced to a
transaction. That rejection has since drawn recurring online pushback: former RSAF pilot Jonathan Tee
publicly called the allowance structure "exploitation" and the new-citizen NS exemption "a betrayal";
The Online Citizen ran a commentary by Terry Xu arguing the "duty" framing dodges a straightforward
pay question, pointing to a 2016 court case where MINDEF's own lawyers argued the allowance "is
neither a salary nor computed as a salary"; a viral Reddit post by a self-described NS officer
regretting his commission reignited the debate over extended legal liability versus pay; and a viral
anecdote about an NSF telling a domestic helper he'd only carry his own pack "in camp" turned into a
wider debate about NS attitudes. All within about a month of the original rejection, so it's still
simmering rather than settled.
