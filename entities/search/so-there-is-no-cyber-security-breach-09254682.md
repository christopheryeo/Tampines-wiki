## Answer
No filed incident/issue for a breach, but the corpus does reference a real attack: in March 2026,
cyber-espionage group UNC3886 (known for advanced persistent threat attacks on critical-services
sectors worldwide) targeted Singapore's critical information infrastructure. The coverage that
followed is entirely about the government's response, not the breach itself — CSA tasked MINDEF's
CSIT with developing home-grown threat-detection tools, already deployed in selected CII systems
with a progressive rollout planned. It ran as a single wave, framed factually/neutrally (not
alarmist), and was picked up by wire syndication (Yahoo News Hong Kong/Malaysia/Singapore/Taiwan)
off the original Straits Times report — it never recurred, spread into new institutional categories
beyond MINDEF/CSA, or drew any critical/opinionated coverage, so it never crossed into the issue
radar's watchlist. It sits as organisation-level coverage (on the CSA and CSIT notes), not as an
issue.
