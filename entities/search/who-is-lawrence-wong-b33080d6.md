## Question
Who is Lawrence Wong?

## Answer
Lawrence Wong is Singapore's Prime Minister and Minister for Finance. The only coverage of him
in the wiki so far: he met Princess Anne of the UK at the Istana on 13 Nov 2025, marking the 60th
anniversary of Singapore–UK diplomatic relations, where they discussed cooperation in trade,
defence, and digital innovation.
