## Summary
Digital and Intelligence Service — Singapore's fourth military branch alongside the army, navy, and air force. Hosts the Work-While-Learning Scheme (WLS) that let NSF [[ammar-abdul-jabar|3SG Ammar Abdul Jabar]] apply his polytechnic cybersecurity training; supported [[csa|CSA]]'s cyberspace-protection efforts. From Jun 2026, NSmen with cyber expertise are to be deployed to the new Sectoral Cyber Defense Team (SCDT).
