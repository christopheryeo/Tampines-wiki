## Summary
British engine manufacturer. Princess Anne visited its Singapore manufacturing facilities on 13 Nov 2025; SVP of Customers John Kelly showcased the company's collaboration with Singapore in civil aviation, defence, power systems, manufacturing, and R&D. In Mar 2026, SVP Asia-Pacific John Kelly said Singapore's defence-supply-chain role would keep growing; supplies engines for the [[rsaf|RSAF]]'s C-130 and G550 aircraft, with fan-blade and MRO capacity both set to grow 40% over five years.
