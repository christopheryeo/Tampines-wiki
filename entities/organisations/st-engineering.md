## Summary
Singapore-listed defence and engineering group. Its AXIOS/ARTOS drones and EagleStrike loitering munition were showcased at Singapore Airshow 2026 as part of Singapore's "high-low mix" drone strategy. Secured S$600 million in international defence contracts in 2025 (double 2024), including a S$470 million Qatar Emiri Land Forces MRO deal announced 27 Feb 2026. Its Marine arm signed an MoU with [[tkms|TKMS]] in Mar 2026 to explore a joint submarine maintenance hub in Singapore supporting the [[rsn|RSN]]'s 218SG-class submarines.
