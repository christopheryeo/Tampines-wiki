## Summary
Singapore's Ministry of Defence. On 30 Nov 2025, issued a Facebook statement denying a rumour that NSF recruits from Pulau Tekong were barred from a McDonald's outlet at White Sands, clarifying that recruits are free to spend book-out time as they wish provided conduct standards are maintained, and that there are no restrictions imposed by [[bmtc|BMTC]]. Also administers the MINDEF/MHA Group Insurance Scheme (via [[singlife|Singlife]]), and was represented by SMS [[zaqy-mohamad|Zaqy Mohamad]] at the Defence Collective Festival and by Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] at Navy@Vivo 2025. In Mar 2026, coordinated with [[mfa|MFA]] on RSAF repatriation flights from Riyadh, confirmed facilitating US forces' facility access under a 1990 MoU, jointly charged [[amos-yee|Amos Yee]] with MHA over NS enlistment offences, jointly clarified with MHA that two Singaporeans' 2016 volunteering with Sar-El did not constitute foreign military service, procured G550 patrol aircraft, opened MMRC 2 at Bedok Camp, launched the SG Defence Volunteer Network and NS SkillsFuture recognition, tasked CSIT with new CII threat-detection tools, fielded a wave of Chan Chun Sing parliamentary written replies (IPPT, madrasah NS, defence expenditure, bullying, EEDS, procurement tenders), hosted DSTA's 26th anniversary and both German and US defence-chief visits, saw outgoing Permanent Secretary (Defence) [[chan-heng-kee|Chan Heng Kee]] promoted to Head of Civil Service, and discussed a joint training facility in [[kalimantan|Kalimantan]] with Indonesia.
