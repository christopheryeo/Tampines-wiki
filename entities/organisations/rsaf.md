## Summary
Republic of Singapore Air Force, a service branch of [[saf|SAF]]. Deployed an A330 Multi-Role Tanker Transport (MRTT) to repatriate Singaporeans from [[riyadh|Riyadh]] in Mar 2026, and contributed F-15SG fighter jets, an A330 MRTT, and a G550 Airborne Early Warning aircraft to the "Cope Tiger 26" exercise at [[korat-air-base|Korat Air Base]], Thailand. Also in Mar 2026: featured in the Poseidon (P-8A) acquisition context, its static/flying display anchored Singapore Airshow 2026, [[rolls-royce|Rolls-Royce]] confirmed it as a key engine customer, 60 new officers commissioned into it, and former Chief [[ng-chee-khern|Ng Chee Khern]] became the new ISEAS-Yusof Ishak Institute director. A former RSAF pilot ([[jonathan-tee|Jonathan Tee]]) publicly criticised Defence Minister Chan Chun Sing's NS remarks.
