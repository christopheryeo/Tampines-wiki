## Summary
ThyssenKrupp Marine Systems, a German naval shipbuilder and supplier of the [[rsn|RSN]]'s 218SG-class submarines (Singapore has ordered six, including two added in 2025). In Mar 2026, TKMS and [[st-engineering|ST Engineering]] Marine signed an MoU to explore a joint submarine maintenance hub in Singapore, witnessed by German Defence Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]] and Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]].
