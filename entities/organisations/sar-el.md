## Summary
Israeli volunteer organisation. Two Singaporeans (a father and son, unnamed) volunteered with it for two weeks in Dec 2016, doing non-combat support work; MHA and MINDEF said in Mar 2026 that their 2016 participation did not constitute foreign military service under Singapore law at the time, but noted Sar-El's website now describes itself as "deeply committed to supporting the [[idf|IDF]]," which would make similar involvement unacceptable today.
