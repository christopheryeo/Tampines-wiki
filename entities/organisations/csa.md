## Summary
Cyber Security Agency of Singapore. On 14 Nov 2025, awarded over S$16 million to 18 research/innovation projects (via the CyberSG R&D Programme Office) and launched CyberCall 2025 at CyberSG Innovation Day, announced by SMS [[tan-kiat-how|Tan Kiat How]]. Also supported by [[dis|DIS]] NSFs under MINDEF's Work-While-Learning Scheme, per Mar 2026 coverage. Tasked [[mindef|MINDEF]]'s [[csit|CSIT]] with developing Singapore's own threat-detection tools following UNC3886 attacks (Mar 2026).
