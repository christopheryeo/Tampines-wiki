## Summary
A [[mindef|MINDEF]] initiative announced by SMS for Defence [[zaqy-mohamad|Zaqy Mohamad]] during the 27 Feb 2026 Committee of Supply debate, bringing volunteers from MINDEF and related bodies (SAFRA, SAF Volunteer Corps) under a single identity. Its inaugural core team comprises about 2,000 members drawn from across these organisations, aiming to broaden civilian pathways to contribute to Total Defence.
