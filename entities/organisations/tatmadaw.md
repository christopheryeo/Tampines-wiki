## Summary
Myanmar's armed forces. Sent a delegation led by General [[kyaw-swa-lin|Kyaw Swa Lin]], Chief of the General Staff, to the 26th [[acamm|ACAMM]] in Singapore (26 Nov 2025), representing the Vice-Chair of the State Security and Peace Commission, Vice-Senior General Soe Win. Its commander since 2011, [[min-aung-hlaing|Min Aung Hlaing]] (who led the 2021 coup), stepped down on 30 Mar 2026 to run for president, handing the commander-in-chief role to [[ye-win-oo|Ye Win Oo]].
