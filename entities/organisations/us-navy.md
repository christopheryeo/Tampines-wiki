## Summary
Rotates littoral combat ships (USS Tulsa, USS Santa Barbara, home-ported in [[bahrain|Bahrain]]) through Singapore for maintenance and logistics, under a Singapore-US agreement allowing this to operate primarily from Singapore as a hub — per [[mindef|MINDEF]]'s Mar 2026 statement citing a 1990 MoU on US facility access. USS Tripoli, part of a naval formation with USS New Orleans and USS San Diego, also transited the Singapore Strait en route to the Middle East amid the US/Israel-Iran conflict.
