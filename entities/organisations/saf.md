## Summary
Singapore Armed Forces. Marked 60 years in 2025, commemorated at the inaugural Defence Collective Festival; also the employer of SAF Regular [[yeo-fu-kiang|Yeo Fu Kiang]], whose recovery from Stage 4 nose cancer was documented. From Jun 2026, to deploy NSmen with cyber expertise to a new Sectoral Cyber Defense Team (SCDT), per Mar 2026 coverage of [[dis|DIS]]'s Work-While-Learning Scheme. Also in Mar 2026: procured G550 patrol aircraft and opened MMRC 2 at Bedok Camp for drone-era training, commissioned 253 new officers, was the subject of a 1991 SQ117 hijack-rescue retrospective and its 35th-anniversary follow-up ([[operation-thunderbolt|Operation Thunderbolt]]), conducted live-firing drills across [[pulau-sudong|Pulau Sudong]] and other ranges, and is the focus of [[dsta|DSTA]]'s 26th-anniversary feature and the ongoing NS allowance-fairness debate.
