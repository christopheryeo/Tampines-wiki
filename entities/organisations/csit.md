## Summary
Centre for Strategic Infocomm Technologies, a technical agency under [[mindef|MINDEF]]. Tasked by [[csa|CSA]] with developing Singapore's own threat-detection tools for critical information infrastructure (CII) owners, following attacks by the cyber-espionage group UNC3886; tools already deployed in selected CII systems in Mar 2026, with progressive rollout planned.
