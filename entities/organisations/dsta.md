## Summary
Defence Science and Technology Agency, [[mindef|MINDEF]]'s technology backbone, formed 15 Mar 2000 from a merger of eight Defence Technology Community entities whose roots trace to 1966. Delivered the Air C2 Hub, the [[saf|SAF]]'s SAR21 Laser Aiming Device, the SARS-era infrared fever-screening system, the Marina Bay Floating Platform, the world's first underground urban ammunition storage facility, the Colt LMG (2024), and Singapore's first autonomous Maritime Security Unmanned Surface Vessel (2025). Its Centre for Strategic Infocomm Technologies ([[csit|CSIT]]) develops Singapore's own cyber threat-detection tools. German Defence Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]] visited DSTA's Quantum Sensing Center during his Mar 2026 Singapore visit.
