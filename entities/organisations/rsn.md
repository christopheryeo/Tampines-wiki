## Summary
Republic of Singapore Navy, a service branch of [[saf|SAF]]. Submariners [[kee-jie-en|ME1 Kee Jie En]] and his father [[kee-kian-peng|SLTC (Ret) Kee Kian Peng]] both served in RSN; its 7th Flotilla supports submariners' families. Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] visited Navy@Vivo 2025 at [[vivocity|VivoCity]], touring RSS Resolution and meeting Chief of Navy [[sean-wat|RADM Sean Wat]]. In Mar 2026, its Digital Transformation Office drove sailor-led low-code innovation, 30 new officers were commissioned into it, [[tkms|TKMS]]/[[st-engineering|ST Engineering]] explored a submarine maintenance hub for its 218SG-class fleet, and it hosted US CNO [[daryl-caudle|Daryl Caudle]]'s introductory visit.
