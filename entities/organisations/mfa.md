## Summary
Singapore's Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Coordinated with [[mindef|MINDEF]] to deploy an [[rsaf|RSAF]] A330 MRTT to repatriate Singaporeans from [[riyadh|Riyadh]] in Mar 2026, contacting registered Singaporeans in the Gulf region with details. Issued a statement of regret after the US-Israel strikes on Iran; urged Singaporeans in the Middle East to stay vigilant and e-register during the repatriation operation.
