## Summary
ASEAN Chiefs of Army Multilateral Meeting. Its 26th edition was held in Singapore on 26 Nov 2025 alongside the 13th ASEAN Sergeant Majors Annual Meeting (ASMAM); Defence Minister [[chan-chun-sing|Chan Chun Sing]] gave the opening speech and army chiefs signed a joint declaration on climate adaptation in HADR operations. Attended by [[tatmadaw|Tatmadaw]] (Myanmar), and army chiefs/reps from [[malaysia|Malaysia]], [[thailand|Thailand]], [[brunei|Brunei]], and [[laos|Laos]], hosted by Singapore's Chief of Army [[cai-dexian|MG Cai Dexian]].
