## Summary
SAF Volunteer Corps, launched 24 Mar 2015 to let those not required to serve NS — women, first-generation PRs, and new citizens — contribute to national defence; one of 30 recommendations from the Committee to Strengthen National Service, adopted by the Government in 2014. Its inaugural intake of 68 volunteers took their oath at Maju Camp under then-Commander COL Mike Tan; marked its 10th anniversary in 2025 having trained 1,500+ volunteers. Feeds into the [[sg-defence-volunteer-network|SG Defence Volunteer Network]] launched in 2026.
