## Summary
Israel Defense Forces. Subject of MHA/MINDEF's Mar 2026 statement clarifying two Singaporeans who volunteered with [[sar-el|Sar-El Volunteer Corps]] in 2016 did not pledge allegiance to, or serve in combat roles for, the IDF. Separately, K.
