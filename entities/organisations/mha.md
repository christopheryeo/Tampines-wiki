## Summary
Ministry of Home Affairs, referred to in coverage as the "Home Team". Covered jointly with [[mindef|MINDEF]] under the Group Insurance Scheme enhanced by [[singlife|Singlife]] in Nov 2025; Home Team personnel and community responders also took part in Exercise Heartbeat, an anti-terror drill at [[clarke-quay|Clarke Quay]]. In Mar 2026, jointly charged [[amos-yee|Amos Yee]] with MINDEF over NS enlistment offences, jointly clarified with MINDEF that two Singaporeans' 2016 Sar-El volunteering wasn't foreign military service, and issued a POFMA correction direction to The Online Citizen over misrepresented remarks by K. Shanmugam on Singaporeans and the IDF.
