## Summary
Service branch of [[saf|SAF]]. [[yeo-fu-kiang|Yeo Fu Kiang]], a Master Technician Trainer who worked with military tanks, signed on with the Singapore Army right after NS; his recovery from Stage 4 nose cancer was documented in Nov 2025. In Mar 2026, 60 new officers commissioned from the Army, and its 48th Battalion, Singapore Armoured Regiment trained with the German Army at [[ex-panzer-strike|Exercise Panzer Strike]]'s 10th-anniversary, 17th-edition iteration (28 Feb-19 Mar 2026), deploying Leopard 2SG tanks and Hunter AFVs.
